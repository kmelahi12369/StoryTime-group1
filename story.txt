I had finally arrived in Berlin. All of a sudden, a Hund wearing lederhose sprinted through the biergarten. 
The hund grabbed a bratwurst and jumped into a porsche speeding away down the hauptstrasse. 
The driver turned up the klimaanlage and made his way to the flughafen, so the hund could catch his flight home to his schloss.
The hund liked to eat his favorite bratwurst at the flughafen while listening to Rammstein.
The hund really hated Berlin.
The Doener was really good, though, so at least there was that.
It was really good with some Brot. 
To celebrate, the hund bought a huge brot and tried to drive in his Porche.
The hund enjoyed the huge brot that he bought and drove safely - a bad driver the hund was not.
But all of the extra activities the hund partook in caused him to miss his flight to his schloss.
Unfortunately, the huge Brot blocked the Hund’s view, and he accidentally crashed his Porsche into a Baeckerei, where a surprised Mehlhase rewarded him with free Gebaeck and Apfelkuchen.
